# Acknowledgements

## Ticket Booth

Some of the Stage 2 design came from reading Ticket Booth by Alessandro Iepure, a GNOME app for tracking films and TV that used the TMDB API.

- Source: https://github.com/aleiepure/ticketbooth
- Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later, copyright (C) 2023-2025 Alessandro Iepure
- Status: archived by its author as end-of-life in April 2026

No Ticket Booth code is in this repository. It's GPL and this project hasn't chosen a licence yet, so I took ideas only and wrote everything from scratch. Ideas aren't covered by copyright, but credit is still owed.

What I took, and what I'm doing differently, is in [docs/prior-art/ticketbooth.md](docs/prior-art/ticketbooth.md).
